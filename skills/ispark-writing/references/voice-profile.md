# Optional Voice Profile

Clean, neutral prose is only a floor. A voice profile may restore an author's real rhythm without turning the company plugin into a store of personal writing.

## When to use

- Read an existing project-local `voice-profile.md` when it is present and authorized.
- Calibrate from user-provided samples when the user asks for a personal voice. Two or three representative paragraphs are enough to observe rhythm; one sentence is not.
- Do not create or persist a profile unless the user explicitly asks for that persistence. Keep the profile in the user's project, outside the plugin source and shared company docs.

## What to observe

Record evidence, not personality guesses:

- approximate short/medium/long sentence mix and use of fragments;
- punctuation and formatting habits, including whether dashes, parentheses, or semicolons occur;
- ordinary transitions, formality, contractions, openings, and sign-offs;
- recurring vocabulary or paragraph density that is appropriate to the requested register.

Ignore quoted, forwarded, boilerplate, or third-party text. Do not infer sensitive traits from style.

## Applying the profile

Use the profile to guide rhythm, diction, and register after the fact ledger is fixed. It never licenses invented facts, fake intimacy, forced informality, or a known clarity problem. When a casual sample conflicts with a formal target artifact, retain useful structural habits and drop casual markers.

If the user says a result does not sound like them, record the concrete correction in the authorized project profile rather than accumulating hidden global memory.
