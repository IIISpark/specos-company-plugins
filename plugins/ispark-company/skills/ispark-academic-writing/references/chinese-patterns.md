# Chinese Academic Pattern Guide

These are review signals, not banned tokens. Keep a phrase when the paper genuinely needs it or the discipline uses it precisely.

## Common signals

- Generic openings such as `随着……的发展`, `近年来……受到广泛关注`, or `在……背景下` when the context does not affect the study.
- Contribution or conclusion inflation such as `具有重要意义`, `填补空白`, `充分证明`, `有效提升`, or `提供有力支撑` without matching evidence.
- Repeated `首先/其次/最后`, `此外/进一步/与此同时`, or `由此可见` when the relation is additive, implicit, or absent.
- Abstract noun chains such as `机制构建`, `体系完善`, `路径优化`, and `能力提升` that hide the actor, operation, object, or measured result.
- Policy or marketing language such as `赋能`, `协同`, `闭环`, `多维度`, and `全面` when it does not name a construct or scope.
- Machine-translated structure: excessive `其`, nested `的`, redundant bilingual labels, or direct English ordering that obscures the subject and relation.
- Results that mix observation with interpretation, or conclusions that expand beyond the tested data and stated limitations.

## Bounded replacements

Replace a signal only when the source supplies the needed detail. For example, `实验结果充分证明` may become `实验结果表明` when the result supports that modality; `进一步验证了有效性` may become a metric-specific observation when the metric is already present. If the source has no detail, shorten or qualify the sentence and flag the missing evidence.

## False positives

- Formality is not slop when the section, venue, or legal/policy requirement needs it.
- A connector is useful when it marks a real cause, contrast, condition, or sequence.
- A repeated technical term is preferable to a synonym that changes the construct.
- `显著` may be statistical terminology only when the method and result support that use; do not remove it mechanically.
- A limitation or cautious hedge is not weak writing. Preserve it when it bounds the claim.
